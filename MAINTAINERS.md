# Maintainers

The people who can merge to `main` and cut releases, and the areas
they own. Changes here are PRs to this file (see GOVERNANCE.md for
the ladder and the criteria).

| Maintainer | Areas | Since |
|---|---|---|
| [@atqatq](https://github.com/atqatq) | everything, while the bus factor is 1 | 2026-08 |

Becoming a maintainer is a public promotion with named criteria —
never a quiet addition to a GitHub team. When the project moves to
its intended long-term home (Eclipse Foundation — see GOVERNANCE.md),
this file becomes the seed of the Eclipse project's maintainer list.
