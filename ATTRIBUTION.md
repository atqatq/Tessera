# Attribution — how to comply

Tessera is open source under Apache-2.0 plus one condition: **any product,
service, deployment, or derivative that uses Tessera must visibly name it**.
Commercial or non-commercial — same rule. This page shows the shortest path
to compliance in common cases; the binding text is the
[LICENSE Appendix](LICENSE#appendix--tessera-naming-requirement) and the
[NOTICE](NOTICE).

## The one-line credit

```
Powered by Tessera - https://github.com/tessera-scm/tessera
```

Pick **one** visible placement (a):

- the primary UI **footer**, or
- the **about dialog** of your application, or
- your product **documentation, website, or distribution README** (b).

Embedded/firmware distributions: include the credit in the accompanying
documentation **and** the device's about/settings screen where present.

## By case

| You are… | Minimum compliance |
|---|---|
| Running Tessera **internally** (no external end users) | keep the credit in source, NOTICE, and internal docs |
| Building an **app or service on Tessera** | "Powered by Tessera" in the UI footer or about dialog, linking to the repo |
| **Forking / redistributing** | retain LICENSE + NOTICE verbatim; keep the credit visible in your UI or docs |
| Shipping an **embedded / OEM** build | credit in docs + device about/settings screen |
| **Re-selling** Tessera-based software | same as app/service — your customers must see the credit; you may add your own branding alongside |
| Writing **papers, slides, or posts** about it | name Tessera and link the repo (good practice, and appreciated) |

## What you may NOT do

- Strip or hide the credit from user-visible surfaces.
- Remove or rewrite the LICENSE appendix or the NOTICE file.
- Present a Tessera derivative as credit-free "white label" software.

## What you MAY do

- Use Tessera commercially, charge for it, bundle it, and build a business
  on it — as long as the name travels with the product.
- Add your own branding next to the credit ("Acme WMS — Powered by Tessera").
- Use the Tessera name solely to identify the origin of the work — the
  LICENSE appendix grants that trademark permission explicitly.
